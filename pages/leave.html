Annual / Earned Leave
20 days per year. Accrual at 1.67 days/month. Carry-forward up to 10 days. Encashment allowed at year end.
Sick Leave
6 days per year. Medical certificate required for 3+ consecutive days. No carry-forward or encashment.
Casual Leave
4 days per year. For short personal needs. Cannot be prefixed or suffixed to a holiday unless approved. No carry-forward.
Comp Off
Granted for approved work on holidays. Must be availed within 30 days. Manager approval required before and after the working day.
Statutory & Special Leave
Maternity leave (26 weeks), paternity leave (15 days), bereavement (5 days), marriage (3 days). Governed by applicable Labour Codes and company policy. These leaves are in addition to the standard entitlements. Contact HR for eligibility and documentation requirements.
